01 About me

I once dreamed of studying medicine. Life had other plans, and that is what shaped my path: from a pharmacy in Douala via a biology degree, curiosity led me to computer science. Healthcare never quite let go of me, though. In the research project Pflegebrille I helped develop AR applications for nursing care, and at HealthHack 2026 I built an AI learning platform for international nursing students.

Today I hold two degrees (M.Sc. in Business Informatics and B.Sc. in Digital Management) and spent more than three years at Forvis Mazars: digitalizing business processes, translating requirements and coaching business users to become citizen developers.

Breaking difficult problems down cleanly, whether as a mathematical model, an AI pipeline or in a workshop with business stakeholders: that is what I enjoy most.

02 My journey

Not a straight career path, but a journey with detours that were worth it.

2015 – 2017

Douala, Cameroon: pharmacy and biology

Assistant at the Pharmacie du Marché and a biology degree at the Université de Douala, dreaming of medical school. In parallel: learning German at the local language center. Two tracks, one goal: out into the world.

Nov. 2017

Arriving in Germany

A fresh start in Berlin, where part of my family lives. Alongside language preparation I volunteered at a kindergarten, my first everyday life in German and to this day a great school in communication.

2018

The Harz mountains become home

After language school I moved to Clausthal-Zellerfeld, and the Upper Harz never let go of me: forest, a small close-knit university and warm-hearted people. I stayed for a degree in business informatics, even though Berlin was calling.

2019

A summer on the assembly line: Daimler, Rastatt

Two months of summer work in production. Short but formative: ever since, I know how manufacturing really works and what process optimization means on the shop floor.

2021 – 2022

Research: Pflegebrille (federally funded)

As a student researcher I developed Android apps and MQTT interfaces for AR glasses in nursing care. Technology meets healthcare, my old passion in a new shape.

2022

Bachelor's thesis: e-waste and the circular economy

A quantitative analysis of return options for waste electrical equipment in Germany. My motivation was personal: the e-waste dumps in Africa where children search old devices for metals instead of going to school. Sustainability became the common thread of my academic work.

2022 – 2026

Forvis Mazars: Digital Solutions

More than three years as a working student: building business applications on low-code platforms, gathering requirements, coaching citizen developers. This is where I learned to translate between business and IT.

2023 – 2026

Two degrees and China

A Master's in business informatics and, in parallel, a B.Sc. in digital management, plus a 2024 summer school at Sichuan University in Chengdu. In 2026 I graduated with a thesis on route planning for electric vehicles.

2026

Freiburg: home at last

I had visited Freiburg several times during my Clausthal years and fell in love with the city: the Black Forest at the doorstep, sunshine, joie de vivre. In 2026 I moved here for good. This is where the next chapter begins: starting my career at the intersection of AI, data and business processes.

03 Common thread: sustainability

From e-waste dumps to electric vehicles: my academic work follows one conviction. Technology has to be part of the solution.

The motivation

E-waste dumps in Africa

Discarded devices from Europe end up on dumps in Africa, with consequences for the environment, health and children's education. This image has shaped my research.

Bachelor's thesis

Circular economy

A quantitative analysis of how and where waste electrical equipment is returned in Germany, and why collection volumes and consumer behavior differ by region.

Master's thesis

Optimizing e-mobility

Route planning for electric vehicles with nonlinear charging functions, so that electric logistics is not only clean but also efficient.
